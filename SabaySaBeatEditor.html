Save Preset snapshots everything — every pad's notes, colors, Duration, Label, and Label Style, Grid Line Color/Thickness, Color Palette, Random Color Per Hit, all five per-pad effect toggles, both Canvas Effects with their Riso Ink Colors, Energy Pulse Intensity, and Label Font Weight/Roundness — under a name you choose. Load Preset opens the list of saved presets: tap Load on one to restore that whole snapshot (it'll ask you to confirm, since it replaces your current setup), tap Update to overwrite that same preset with whatever your current pad mappings and settings are right now (keeping its name), or Delete to remove one you no longer need. Handy for switching between different gigs or songs that call for a completely different pad layout and look. Preset Cycler automates that switching: tick the checkbox next to any saved presets you want in rotation, set an Interval (sec), and tap Start Cycling — it applies the first checked preset immediately, then automatically advances to the next one (looping back to the start) every interval, showing what's currently active and its position in the rotation next to the Presets row. Tap Stop Cycling to end it. Deleting a preset that's checked for the cycle removes it from the rotation automatically. Your checked presets and interval are remembered between reloads, but the cycler always starts Off, like the effect toggles. Programs, at the top of the same Preset Cycler window, let you save that whole checkbox selection and interval as a named setup — tap Save as Program once you've got the rotation you want. Tap Load on a saved program to restore its exact selection and interval (skipping any presets that have since been deleted), tap Update to overwrite it with whatever's currently checked and whatever interval is set, then tap Start Cycling to run it — handy for keeping several different rotations ready to go, like one per set or one per style of song. Delete removes a program without touching the presets it referenced. Export, on both a saved preset and a saved program, downloads a self-contained JSON file with everything needed to play it — handy for the companion Player app, or just as a backup. Preset Transition Duration sets how long Grid Line Color/Thickness and Riso Ink Colors take to smoothly animate whenever they change — most noticeable when a preset loads or the Cycler advances, but it applies to manual edits too. Drag to 0 for instant snapping, up to 3 full seconds for a slow fade, or tap Reset for the default 500ms. Since it's saved as part of each preset, different presets can each carry their own transition feel — a fast cut for one, a slow crossfade for another.

Tap + Note on a pad — it stays armed, so you can hit that same physical pad on each of your banks in turn and every note gets added to it. Tap + Note again to stop listening. Each mapped note shows as a chip; tap its × to remove just that one, or the card's × to clear all of them. Tap the color swatch to pick that pad's color. Pads light up at full brightness on hit and fade back out on release — the fade-out length is tied to the beat length at the current Tempo (kept between 0.5s and 1.5s, so it always feels musical even at extreme tempos), not to how long you held the pad. For pads that trigger a longer song snippet rather than a one-shot, set Duration (beats) to how many beats that clip lasts — the pad then stays lit for that many beats at the current tempo, regardless of how briefly you tapped it, and re-hitting it restarts the countdown. Leave Duration blank for normal hold-based behavior.

Each pad card has a Label field (up to 12 characters) — whatever you type shows as text centered on that pad, but only while it's actually lit: the label fades in the instant a pad is hit and fades out at the exact same moment and speed as the pad's own light, whether that's a quick hold-based fade or a full song snippet on a Duration pad. Leave it blank for no text at all. Each pad also has its own Label Style — a color swatch and a size (px) — so different pads can have differently styled text. Under Set All Label Colors and Set All Label Sizes, picking a color or dragging the slider applies it to every pad at once (handy for starting from a consistent look before customizing individual pads), and either Reset All puts every pad back to white at 28px. Label text always renders in the custom embedded typeface (Doto, a dotted variable font). Under 🔲 Re-Grid, a label's size continuously scales with its pad's actual on-screen shape — enlarge a pad and its text grows to match, shrink it and the text shrinks too, smoothly following the same animation, with each pad's own size acting as a relative scale rather than a fixed number of pixels. Label Font Weight (100–900) and Label Font Roundness (0–100) control that typeface's own variable axes for every label at once — drag either slider to see the dots thicken/thin or sharpen/round in real time, or tap Reset to go back to 700 weight, 0 roundness. Both persist between reloads.

Tempo is tracked live from your own mapped pad hits (median of recent intervals, so a stray hit won't throw it off) — tap Reset Tempo when switching to a song with a very different tempo so old intervals don't linger. Several other settings key off this tracked tempo: pad fade-out length, Energy Pulse's shimmer speed, and Duration (beats) pads.

Toggle 🎲 Random Color Per Hit to have each pad pick a new random color every time it's struck — its swatch updates live to reflect the color from its most recent hit. Grid Line Color sets the color of the gaps between pads on the live grid (defaults to black) — tap the swatch to pick a color, or Reset to go back to black. Neighbor Splash and Energy Pulse's shimmer always blend against black regardless of this setting, so changing it never tints those effects. Grid Line Thickness adjusts the gap between pads on the live grid, from hairline to thick — drag the slider or tap Reset for the default 6px. Color Palette constrains every random color — Random Color Per Hit, Combo Rainbow — to a set of up to 10 specific colors instead of the full rainbow: tap + Color, then tap the new swatch to pick its exact color the same way you pick a pad's color. Combo Rainbow cycles through your palette in order along a chain instead of stepping evenly around the hue wheel. Tap a chip's × to remove it, or Full Rainbow to clear the whole palette and go back to unconstrained random. With no colors set, nothing changes from before.

Five extra effects, each toggled independently so you can mix and match: 🔥 Heat Intensity desaturates a pad toward gray when it's been quiet and brings it to full color the more you've hit it recently — though a pad that dominates your overall playing (a one-shot drum hit every beat, say) has that heat automatically dampened over the last minute of play, so it doesn't perpetually max out while pads you touch less often barely register; this fairness adjustment applies to every effect that reads heat, including Re-Grid's growth. 💦 Neighbor Splash spills a brief tint onto a pad's idle neighbors as soon as you hit it twice in quick succession. 🌊 Energy Pulse makes every idle pad shimmer in time with your tracked Tempo once one's been established — it keeps shimmering even if you stop playing, using the last known BPM, and only stops if you turn the toggle off or tap Reset Tempo. Whichever pad you hit most recently shimmers noticeably brighter than the rest once it settles back to idle, so it's always easy to follow which pad was played last even after its flash has faded. Its Energy Pulse Intensity toggle sets how strong that shimmer is — 0.5× for a subtler flicker, 2× for a much more dramatic swing, 1× is the original strength. 🌈 Combo Rainbow builds a chain when you hit neighboring pads in a straight line along a row or column in quick succession — every pad currently in the chain relights together with a gradient across that line and stays lit as a group, extending each time the chain grows, until the chain goes quiet. 🔲 Re-Grid reflows the whole grid on hit — the struck pad's entire row and entire column change size (squeezing or expanding the rest of the grid to compensate), the effect growing stronger the more frequently that pad's been played. Re-Grid Mode decides which way: Enlarge (the original behavior) grows the hit pad's row/column bigger than the rest; Shrink does the opposite, making the hit pad's row/column smaller so the rest of the grid gets relatively bigger; Alternate flips between the two on every single hit anywhere, regardless of which pad. Whichever mode is picked, the shape holds as-is until you hit another pad, which reflows it again for that pad instead — turning Re-Grid off snaps the layout back to an even 4×4. Re-Grid Mode itself persists between reloads, even though the Re-Grid toggle doesn't. None of these five toggle states are remembered between reloads, so they always start Off.

Two full-screen effects layered over the entire live view, not tied to any single pad — mix either with the per-pad effects above freely. 🖨 Riso Print mimics a risograph print: two slightly offset ink-colored layers tint everything as a duotone — re-hueing the image while preserving its original brightness, so blacks stay black rather than picking up a color cast — plus a fine halftone dot pattern for that characteristic mis-registered, dot-printed look. A contrast/saturation/brightness boost is baked into the effect so it reads as punchy rather than muddy. Riso Ink Colors lets you pick both ink colors yourself the same way you pick a pad's color — tap either swatch, or Reset to go back to the default pink and blue. Unlike the effect toggles themselves, your chosen ink colors do persist between reloads. 🌫 Grain overlays a flickering film-grain texture across the whole screen, cycling through a handful of noise frames for an organic, unsteady feel rather than a static overlay. Like the per-pad effects, neither of the two Canvas Effects toggle states is remembered between reloads.

Loops a video or GIF from your device as a full-screen backdrop, sitting behind everything else — the grid, its effects, and Canvas Effects all still render on top of it. Tap Choose Video/GIF to pick a file (it plays locally, nothing is uploaded anywhere) and tap Off/On to show or hide it — the toggle needs a file loaded first. Remove clears the loaded file entirely and turns the overlay off. Video Opacity controls how bright/visible the video itself is. Grid Transparency controls how see-through idle pads become so the video shows through them — pads you're actually hitting always stay fully opaque so the performance colors read clearly against the video; only idle pads and the gaps between them reveal it. While the overlay is on, it also ties Riso Print's ink layers, Grain, Neighbor Splash's idle tint, and Energy Pulse's idle shimmer to that same opacity value, so every one of those fades together in step with the grid instead of each sitting at its own independent strength — turning the overlay off returns all of them to their normal fixed opacity. Heat Intensity, Combo Rainbow, and Re-Grid aren't affected, since they only ever act on pads you're actually hitting, which always stay fully opaque regardless. It also temporarily takes over from Grid Line Color in the gaps, since the two can't be visible in the same space — Grid Line Color reappears normally once the overlay's off. The loaded file, its opacity, and its transparency setting all persist between reloads, but the on/off toggle itself doesn't, like the other effects. The overlay toggle, opacity, and transparency are included in Save/Load Preset — the file itself isn't, since duplicating a video into every preset would get heavy fast.
